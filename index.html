Manufacturing Excellence

India's Premier Uniform Manufacturing Partner

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, Planet10 has established itself as a leading uniform manufacturer serving institutions and enterprises across India. Our integrated approach encompasses custom design, premium fabric sourcing, precision embroidery, advanced printing, and efficient large-scale production.

We serve clients ranging from local schools to national hospitality chains, maintaining consistent quality across all order volumes. Our manufacturing infrastructure and experienced team enable us to meet demanding timelines while delivering uniforms that exceed industry standards.
