About Planet10

India's trusted partner for comprehensive uniform manufacturing solutions.

Our Story

Crafting Excellence Since 2014

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, Planet10 Uniforms has established itself as a leading uniform manufacturing partner serving institutions and enterprises across India.

Our integrated approach encompasses the entire uniform lifecycle — from initial design consultation and fabric sourcing to precision embroidery, advanced printing, quality control, and nationwide delivery.

Whether you're launching a school uniform program, outfitting a healthcare facility, or establishing corporate brand identity, Planet10 delivers consistent quality at scale with dedicated account management throughout the process.
